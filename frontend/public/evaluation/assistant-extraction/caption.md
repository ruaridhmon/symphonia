# Figure: Faithfulness does not guarantee coverage

**Exploratory assistant extraction pilot; provisional self-review.**

**a, Coverage and faithfulness.** Coverage is the number of original focal-claim occurrences fully preserved divided by the number of original focal-claim occurrences in a panel. Faithfulness is the number of fully supported extracted claims divided by the number of extracted claims. Circle areas and numerals encode the number of panels with identical scores; five coordinates represent all 24 panels. Soft halos are decorative, not density estimates or uncertainty intervals. Marginal marks count panels at each observed score. Axes are cropped and include every observed value. Dotted lines show the algebraic constraints imposed by eight outputs and nine or ten reference propositions, not fitted relationships. Seventeen panels have 100% faithfulness and 80–88.9% coverage.

**b, Retention across scenarios.** Six disjoint two-response panels belong to each of four synthetic scenarios. Purple line segments represent fully preserved focal claims, amber segments partially preserved claims and grey segments omitted claims. Filled markers indicate strict coverage; open markers add partial preservation. These segments are not confidence intervals. Percentages beside scenario headings are pooled faithful counts divided by pooled reference counts: 43/55, 46/55, 45/55 and 48/55. These are descriptive differences, not independent replications or evidence of domain-specific effects.

**c, Aggregate outcomes.** Across 220 focal-claim occurrences, 182 were labelled fully preserved (82.7%), 10 partially preserved (4.5%) and 28 omitted (12.7%). The denominator counts panel-level occurrences of preassigned focal propositions, not all possible meanings in the text or 220 independent observations. Rounding explains the displayed total of 99.9%.

**Design and limitations.** Forty-eight previously generated synthetic source responses were paired without reuse into 24 panels. Each extraction was limited to eight claims and 200 words. All panels produced eight claims; the eight-claim limit accounts for the 28 omissions, so this experiment cannot estimate unconstrained coverage. Ten partial labels concern population restrictions (5), broadened outcomes (3), observation period (1) and stance (1). Outputs and labels were produced by the same assistant in one conversation with reference IDs visible. No independent blinded review, real-expert validation or population-level inference is claimed. Some outputs combine respondents; no separate before/after grouping outputs exist, so grouping-specific loss is not identified. No new OpenRouter calls were made.

**Reproduction.** The downloaded bundle contains saved inputs, outputs, explicit judgments and the scoring/plotting scripts. Underlying data have not changed in this design revision.
