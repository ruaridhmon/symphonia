# Symphonia product description

Symphonia helps facilitators collect expert viewpoints, synthesise them into traceable claims, and test those claims in later survey rounds.

## Core workflow

1. Experts answer open questions in their own words.
2. AI extracts distinct claims without erasing disagreement or uncertainty.
3. Each claim retains the exact supporting and opposing expert statements.
4. Facilitators review, edit, and publish claims into a survey.
5. Experts rate the claims in the next round.
6. The summary shows response counts, agreement status, and expandable source statements.

## Evidence rule

A claim must keep its source statements and expert attribution when it is published into another round or survey. Synthetic statements are allowed only in explicitly labelled demos and must always be marked synthetic.

## Environments

- `develop` deploys the dev site.
- `main` deploys production.
